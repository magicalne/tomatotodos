# Privacy

**All Tomato Todos data stays on your device.**

- Tasks, sessions, settings, and timer state are stored **only** in your browser's `localStorage` under the `tomato-todos:*` keys.
- The app makes **no network requests**: no accounts, no analytics, no CDNs, no tracking. Once the page is loaded, it works fully offline.
- The only files you can produce leave via your own explicit action: **Export JSON**, **Download Logseq log**, and clipboard copy — you choose where they go.
- Desktop notifications (optional, off by default) are generated locally by your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Clearing your browser data for the site, or pressing **Wipe everything** in Settings, permanently deletes everything the app knows.
